AI Voice Assistant - Note Taker

Welcome! This is a demo and prototype AI Note Taker. It may make mistakes sometimes.
To take a note, say "Start Note" or click the button below.
The assistant will listen and convert your speech to text.
When you're done, your note will appear below and can be saved locally.

Tips for best results:
  • Wear earphones and keep the mic close to your mouth.
  • Speak clearly and avoid background noise.
  • Record at least 2-3 seconds of audio.
  • Check your microphone permissions.
